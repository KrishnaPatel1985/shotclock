# City of San Clemente: Accessory Dwelling Unit Information Packet (August 2024)

Source (official): https://san-clemente.org/home/showpublisheddocument/86302/638600032255670000
Includes Attachment 6: San Clemente Municipal Code § 17.28.270. Checked: 2026-11-07.
Extracted summary of the city's own rules; verify against the official PDF. Note: HCD's Oct 14, 2025 letter found
several of these rules conflict with state law (see docs/state/hcd-findings-san-clemente-2025-10.md).

## Submittal and permit steps
- Coastal Zone: Coastal Commission approval required (start with City in-concept application).
- HOA approval if applicable.
- City ADU prescreen (evidence of Coastal and HOA approval required) before building permit processing.
- Building and grading permits submitted electronically; see the Building Permit Application for submittal requirements.
- Address exhibit: "Provide site plan, identify all unit/suite entrances and their addresses."
- Onsite wastewater: percolation test within the last five years (10 if recertified).
- Deed restriction: record with the County of Orange Clerk-Recorder "before a building permit may be issued"; notarized; PDF to assigned planner; two copies if ADU and JADU.
- Demolition permit for a detached garage replaced by an ADU is reviewed and issued with the ADU.

## Development standards (city)
- Size: "No development standards will require an ADU to be less than 800 sq. ft." Detached max 850 sq ft (studio/1-bed) or 1,000 sq ft (2-bed). Attached: lesser of 50% of existing living area or those caps. Converted: no size limit. JADU max 500 sq ft.
- Lot coverage: "No ADU greater than 800 sf may cause the total lot coverage to exceed 50%."
- Front setback: zone front setback; no closer to the front line than the primary dwelling's living area.
- Side and rear setbacks: 4 feet.
- Separation from primary building: 5 feet (including eaves).
- Height: attached up to 25 ft (zone limit), max two stories; detached 16 ft, or 18 ft within one-half mile of a qualifying transit stop, plus up to 2 ft to match roof pitch.
- Parking: one space per ADU (tandem/setback OK). None required within one-half mile of public transit, in a qualifying historic district, part of primary residence/accessory structure, on-street permits required but not offered, or a **posted** car-share location within one block. No replacement parking when a garage is demolished/converted.
- Separate bathroom (JADU may share); independent exterior entrance.
- Quantity: single-family lot = one JADU plus one detached or attached ADU. Multifamily = converted up to 25% of units (at least one per building); up to **two** detached ADUs.
- Fire sprinklers only if required in the primary residence.
- Efficiency unit minimum: 220 sq ft plus 100 sq ft per occupant beyond two; closet, kitchen sink, cooking appliance, refrigeration.
- Architecture (§ 17.28.270 F.7): exterior materials, colors, windows and doors **must match** the primary dwelling; roof slope must match; down-lights only; interior at least 10 ft wide in every direction, 7 ft walls; screening allowed.
- Driveway: ADU and primary dwelling share the same driveway unless fire authority requires otherwise.
- Rental term: 30 days or longer (no short-term rentals).
- Ownership: ADU/JADU may not be sold separately.
- Owner occupancy: not required for ADUs created Jan 1, 2020 to Jan 1, 2025; JADU requires owner occupancy; **§ 17.28.270 E.7.b: ADUs permitted on or after Jan 1, 2025 are subject to owner occupancy.**
- Deed restriction recorded before building permit; runs with the land.
- Review time: "60 days when a complete application is submitted."
- Non-conforming zoning conditions need not be corrected.
- ADUs not meeting objective standards may be allowed with an Architectural Permit (§ 17.28.270 H).

## Fees
- No impact fee for ADUs under 750 sq ft (§ 17.28.270 D.4.a); larger ones proportional.
- Utility connection fees: not for converted ADUs on single-family lots; required for detached/new-construction ADUs (capped at reasonable cost).
- ADU permit processing fee set by City Council resolution (no amount in packet).
- Deed restriction exempt from recording fees (Gov. Code § 27383).
