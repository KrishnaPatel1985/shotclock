# HCD findings: City of San Clemente ADU ordinance (letter dated October 14, 2025)

Source (official): https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/ordinance-review-letters/san-clemente-adu-findings-101425.pdf
Summary prepared for this project from the letter. Always link the original above.

State ADU law is now Government Code §§ 66310–66342 (moved there by SB 477; old §§ 65852.2, 65852.22, 65852.26 were deleted).

| # | City rule (San Clemente Muni. Code) | Law HCD cited | What state law says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cites deleted sections 65852.2 / 65852.22 / 65852.26 | Gov. Code §§ 66310–66342 | Must cite the new sections |
| 2 | § 17.28.270(D)(1)(a): one ADU + one JADU, not all combinations | § 66323(a), (a)(2) | Must allow any combination: converted ADU + detached ADU (4-ft side/rear setbacks) + JADU on a single-family lot |
| 3 | § 17.28.270(D)(1)(d): max 2 detached ADUs on multifamily lot | § 66323(a)(4)(A) | Up to 8 detached ADUs (not more than existing units) on existing multifamily lots |
| 4 | § 17.28.270(E)(2)(a): detached ADU height capped at 16 ft | § 66321(b)(4)(B), (b)(4)(C) | 18 ft allowed within 1/2 mile walking distance of major transit stop / high-quality transit corridor (+2 ft for roof pitch to match primary), and on lots with multistory multifamily |
| 5 | § 17.28.270(E)(7): owner occupancy required for units built after Jan 1, 2025 | § 66315; AB 976 (2023) | No owner-occupancy requirement for ADUs; only rentals of 30+ days may be required |
| 6 | § 17.28.270(E)(8): recorded deed restriction for ADUs and JADUs | § 66315 | Deed restriction not allowed for ADUs (required only for JADUs) |
| 7 | § 17.28.270(F)(2)(c)(v): parking waived only if "city-sanctioned, posted" car share within one block | § 66322(a)(5) | No parking can be required when a car share vehicle is within one block; no "sanctioned/posted" qualifier |
| 8 | § 17.28.270(F)(4)(c): lot coverage etc. may limit ADU size | § 66321(b)(3) | Cannot use lot coverage, FAR, open space, minimum lot size, or percentage limits to block an 800 sq ft ADU with 4-ft side/rear setbacks |
| 9 | § 17.28.270(F)(7)(a),(f): materials, colors, windows must "match" primary dwelling | § 66314(b); § 66313(i) | Only objective standards allowed; "match" is subjective |
| 10 | § 17.28.270(G)(2): unpermitted-unit pathway only for units before 2018 | § 66332(a) | Pathway must cover unpermitted units created before 2020 |

City options per HCD: amend the ordinance, or adopt findings explaining why it complies (Gov. Code § 66326(b)(2)).
